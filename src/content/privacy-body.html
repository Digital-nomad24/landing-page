Section 01Who we are

The Convyo mobile application and platform are operated by Convyo Private Limited (“we”, “us”, “the Company”). We are the data fiduciary for the personal data described in this policy.

Registered address: Gali Sangam Wali, Bhagat Singh Colony, Sirsa, Haryana 125055, India.

This policy applies to the App, its communities, rides, events and messaging features, and to this website.

Section 02What we collect

  • Personal information. Basic account data such as your name, phone number, email address, and profile photo. These are considered personal data under Indian law and are used for account setup, authentication, and profile display.
  • User-generated content. Any media or content you upload — photos, videos, and text for events or posts. You retain ownership of this content; the licence you grant us to display it is set out in Section 5 of the Terms.
  • Device information. Technical data such as your device model, operating system version, and IP address, collected automatically to ensure compatibility and security. IP addresses may also be used to approximate location where GPS is unavailable.
  • Location data. With your explicit consent, real-time GPS location, to enable event check-ins, “find nearby riders”, live convoy positions, and navigation. If you deny location access, these features are disabled.
  • Sensor data. With your consent, motion and gyroscope data used to verify presence or activity during rides and events (for example, confirming you attended a ride). This is treated as personal data requiring consent.
  • Community metadata. Membership of communities, RSVPs, roll-call check-ins, and limited metadata such as location relevance, which may be visible to other members of the same community in accordance with your privacy settings.

Section 03Why we use it, and on what basis

We use your data solely to operate the App and provide its services. Your phone number and email enable account creation and notifications; location data enables finding and joining nearby events and keeping a convoy together; sensor data enhances event tracking and safety verification.

All personal data processing is carried out on the basis of your consent or another lawful purpose such as performance of a contract. We follow the principles of purpose limitation and data minimization — we collect only the data needed for each stated purpose.

Consent and withdrawal

We obtain clear, informed consent before collecting sensitive data such as location or motion-sensor data. Consent must be free, specific, informed and unambiguous. You may withdraw consent at any time through your account settings or by contacting support. Withdrawing consent disables the related feature — for example, disabling location access stops real-time mapping.

Section 04Device permissions

The App asks for the following device permissions. Each request is presented clearly and requires your affirmative consent; you can refuse or later revoke any of them in your device settings.

  • Location — community matching, showing rides on a map, geofencing events, live convoy position.
  • Camera / microphone — taking or uploading photos and videos, and optionally audio/video calls or voice messages.
  • Storage — saving and loading your media files on the device and sharing them.
  • Motion & gyroscope — activity recognition (for example, bike movement detection) and presence checks.

Denying permissions will not affect your ability to use core community and messaging features, but location-based and sensor-based functionality will be disabled.

Section 05Sharing and disclosure

  • Within your communities. Member names, profile photos, and limited metadata may be visible to other members of a community you join, in accordance with your privacy settings and the configuration set by that community’s administrators.
  • Service providers. We share data with third parties only as needed to run the service — for example cloud storage, analytics, or a payment processor for in-app purchases. All such sharing is governed by binding Data Sharing Agreements as required under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act.
  • Event partners and sponsors. Where you interact with a third-party event, the organizer may receive certain data such as your RSVP. This access is strictly limited to the stated purpose, and partners must comply with applicable privacy laws.
  • Ride Creators. Rides and paid rides are organised by users, clubs and organisations, not by us. What you share with a Ride Creator — including any payment made directly to them — is outside our control. See Section 8A of the Terms.
  • Legal disclosure. We may disclose data where required by law, regulation, court order, or a lawful request from a government authority.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers. Where advertising is shown, advertisers do not receive your personal contact data unless you explicitly share it; only non-identifying data such as approximate location, device type or usage statistics may be used, in accordance with your consent.

Section 06Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • Erase your data by deleting your content or your account;
  • Withdraw consent for location, sensor, camera, microphone or storage access at any time;
  • Complain to us, and thereafter to the relevant data protection authority.

You can exercise most of these rights directly in the App under account settings. For anything else, write to us at the address in Section 9 and we will respond within the period required by applicable law.

Section 07Retention and deletion

You may delete your account at any time by following the in-app instructions. On deletion, your personal data is erased as described here, and content you uploaded may be immediately removed or anonymized, subject to legal requirements. Clear any unused in-app credits or pending transactions before closing your account.

We cease processing or delete personal data as soon as it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for. If you close your account or withdraw consent, we erase or anonymize your data unless retention is required by law — for example, tax and accounting records. Personal data is not held for longer than necessary.

Remnants of your data may remain in backup archives for a reasonable period for security, disaster recovery, or legal compliance.

Location history is not stored beyond what is needed for real-time features. Location tracking stops when the App is closed or the permission is revoked.

Section 08Data security

  • Security measures. We implement industry-standard security practices, including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/SSL) and at rest, in secure databases and cloud storage. Access to databases and servers is strictly controlled and audited.
  • Legal compliance. In accordance with Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000, we maintain reasonable security practices and procedures to protect sensitive personal data, and adhere to the IT (SPDI) Rules, 2011.
  • Storage. Personal data is stored on secure servers located in India or in compliant regions. Payment information is handled by PCI-compliant processors; we do not store full payment card data on our servers. Authentication credentials are hashed.
  • Breach notification. In the unlikely event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and the relevant authorities in accordance with applicable law.

Section 09Changes and how to reach us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in-app or by email. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
