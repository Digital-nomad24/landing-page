Section 01App Functionality

Community Creation & Management: Users of the Riding Verse (RV) mobile application may create and manage virtual communities centred around bike riding interests, locations, or affiliations (e.g., Cities, States, neighbourhoods, biking clubs, or ride-specific groups). Each community has administrators who can add/remove members, approve content, and set privacy (public or private). Each community created on the platform is subject to the following terms:

  • Creation Rights: Any registered user may initiate a new community, provided they agree to serve as the initial administrator and abide by the app’s community guidelines.
  • Administrative Roles: Community administrators (admins) have elevated privileges, including the ability to:
    • Add or remove members (via invitations, phone number, or join requests)
    • Approve or moderate content and media shared within the group
    • Set or change community privacy status (public or private)
    • Assign additional administrators or moderators
  • Membership Rules: Users may join communities by invitation, phone number lookup, QR code, or based on proximity/location-based recommendations (with explicit user permission).
  • Privacy Settings:
    • Public Communities are visible to all users and may allow open joining or joining via request.
    • Private Communities are hidden from public listings and accessible only through direct invitation or admin approval.
  • Data Display Within Communities: Member names, profile photos, and limited metadata (e.g., location relevance) may be visible within the group to foster trusted interaction, in accordance with the user’s privacy settings.

Community activities (including shared media, messages, and events) are governed by this policy and the community-specific privacy configurations set by its administrators.

Invitations & Members

Members join via invitation links or by sharing their phone number. By providing contact information, you consent to the app using it to connect you to communities.

Events (Public / Private & Third-Party)

Users can host public or private biking events (rides, meet-ups) and invite community members. The App also lists third-party events (e.g. organized by partners). RSVP and ticketing features use collected data for communication.

Real-Time Messaging

Within each community, members can send messages and announcements. Push notifications and in-app chats keep participants informed. Announcements may be broadcast by admins to all community members.

Section 02Data Collection

  • Personal Information: We collect basic account data such as your name, phone number, email address, and profile photo. These are considered personal data under Indian law (e.g. names, addresses, and phone numbers are listed as personal). This information is used for account setup, authentication, and profile display.
  • User-Generated Content: Any media or content you upload (photos, videos, text for events or posts) is collected. You retain ownership of this content, but by uploading you grant us license to use it (see Intellectual Property below).
  • Device Information: Technical data such as your device model, operating system version, and IP address are collected automatically. This helps us ensure compatibility and security. IP addresses are also used to approximate location if GPS is unavailable.
  • Location Data: With your explicit consent, the App collects real-time GPS location to enable features like event check-ins and “find nearby riders.” You must grant location permission in your device settings for these features. If you deny location access, location-based features (community matching, navigation to events) will be disabled.
  • Sensor Data: With consent, we collect motion and gyroscope data to verify presence or activity during rides and Events (e.g. confirming you attended a ride). This data is used only to enhance features like safety verification and is treated as personal data requiring consent.
